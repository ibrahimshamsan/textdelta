How to Compare Two Texts Online: Step-by-Step Guide

Comparing two versions of a text is a common task for developers, writers, students, and professionals. Whether you are checking edits in a document, reviewing code changes, or verifying a translation, an online text comparison tool makes the job fast and accurate. This guide shows you exactly how to compare two texts using TextDelta in four simple steps.

Step 1: Open the TextDelta Tool

Go to the TextDelta homepage. You will see two large text areas side by side. The left area is for the original text, and the right area is for the modified text. No registration, no installation, and no file uploads are required.

Step 2: Paste Your Original Text

Copy the first version of your text and paste it into the left box. This is your reference point. It can be a paragraph, a code snippet, or an entire document. The tool handles text of any length.

Step 3: Paste Your Modified Text

Copy the second version and paste it into the right box. This is the text you want to compare against the original. Once both boxes are filled, you are ready to proceed.

Step 4: Click Compare and Analyze the Results

Click the "Compare Texts" button. TextDelta will instantly highlight every difference. Deletions appear in red on the left side, and additions appear in green on the right side. You can switch between side-by-side view and unified view, and you can copy the result with one click.

Why Use an Online Tool Instead of Desktop Software

Desktop diff software often requires installation, configuration, and a paid license. An online text comparison tool like TextDelta works in any browser, on any device, and is completely free. It also processes everything locally in your browser, which means your text never leaves your computer. This makes it both convenient and secure.

Tips for Accurate Text Comparison

  • Always paste the older version on the left and the newer version on the right.
  • Use the unified view if you want to read the changes in a single continuous flow.
  • Use the side-by-side view if you want to see exactly what was removed and what replaced it.
  • Copy the result and save it as a record of changes if needed.
