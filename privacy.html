Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

TextDelta ("we", "our", "the Service") provides a free online text comparison tool available at https://textdelta-red.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Your Content Stays in Your Browser

TextDelta is designed to process text directly inside your browser using JavaScript. The text you paste for comparison is never transmitted to our servers, never stored, and never logged. We have no ability to read the content you compare. All comparison results are generated on your page and discarded when you close or navigate away from the page.

You remain responsible for the lawfulness of the content you choose to compare. Do not paste content you do not have the right to review.

3. Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit the Service, the following technical information may be collected automatically by us or by third-party services:

  • Browser type and version, operating system, and device type.
  • Referring URL, pages viewed on TextDelta, and language preference.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (country/region level).
  • Aggregated usage statistics such as page load time.

We do not ask for, and the Service does not require, any account, name, postal address, phone number, or payment information.

4. Third-Party Services

Google AdSense (Advertising)

The Service may display advertisements served by Google AdSense (Google LLC). Google and its certified third-party vendors may use cookies, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can learn more or opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Advertising Policies or Google Ads Settings.

Analytics

The Service may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and understand which pages are useful. These analytics are configured to collect aggregated, anonymized information only. They do not use third-party cookies and do not track you across other websites.

Hosting

The Service is delivered from standard web hosting infrastructure (Vercel). The hosting provider may keep short-lived server logs for error diagnosis and abuse prevention.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

The Service may use the following categories of cookies and local storage:

  • Essential: to remember your preferred language.
  • Analytics: to count page views without double-counting.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense and its certified vendors may set cookies to serve and measure advertising.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us through our GitHub repository.
